# ROAM — أوامر جيميناي (Nano Banana Pro)

## البرومبت الأساسي (ثابت لكل المفاهيم)

```
You are the creative director of a world-class packaging studio (Pentagram / Landor / Futura level) art-directing the hero product shot for ROAM, a specialty coffee-bean brand from the Gulf.
REFERENCE IMAGE = current design. KEEP EXACTLY: the 250g frosted matte translucent stand-up pouch with zip-lock, roasted coffee beans visible through the frosted film, the cream/sand ink colour (#EAD9BF) on dark coffee beans, the SAME label sticker (cream paper, wavy die-cut top/bottom edges, Colombian flag circle at top centre, 'COLOMBIA / CHELCHELE' left, Arabic 'كولومبيا / شيلشلي' right, dashed line, tasting notes 'Berry, Chocolate, Strawberry, Honey', 'Irrigation : Heirloom / Processing : Natural'), and the brand name ROAM in the same tall high-contrast condensed serif.
CHANGE: the printed graphic pattern and the placement/scale of the ROAM wordmark, following the concept below. Everything is printed in a single cream ink directly on the frosted film, so beans show through any unprinted area.
Photography: premium editorial product photo, one pouch, soft directional window light, warm beige linen backdrop, shallow depth of field, crisp legible typography, photoreal, 8k. No extra text beyond ROAM and the label. Spell ROAM correctly.
CONCEPT:
```

## المفاهيم الـ 12

### 01_giant_crop
```
THE GIANT. ROAM set enormous — each letter taller than the pouch — so it bleeds off all four edges; only fragments of R, O, A, M are visible, stacked in two rows (RO / AM). The beans glow through the letter counters. The label sits on top like a passport photo. Confident, fashion-house energy.
```

### 02_knockout_window
```
THE WINDOW. Flip the logic: the whole lower two-thirds of the pouch is flooded solid opaque cream ink, and ROAM is KNOCKED OUT as clear film, so the coffee beans themselves form the letters ROAM. Huge, horizontal, edge to edge. The product is the logo.
```

### 03_topographic_route
```
THE ROUTE. A fine-line topographic contour map pattern (like mountain elevations of a coffee farm) covers the lower bag, with a dotted travel route winding from the bottom-left corner up to a small pin under the label. ROAM is small, elegant, set horizontally at the very bottom centre like a map legend.
```

### 04_vertical_spine
```
THE SPINE. ROAM runs vertically, very tall and narrow, hugging the right edge of the pouch and wrapping around the side gusset like a book spine. The rest of the face is calm and empty frosted film with a single thin horizontal cream line under the label. Minimal Japanese restraint.
```

### 05_arabic_latin_lockup
```
THE BRIDGE. A bold bilingual monogram: Latin ROAM and Arabic 'روم' in matching high-contrast calligraphic stroke style interlocked into one ligature mark, large and centred on the lower bag. Around it, a thin circular orbit ring. East meets West.
```

### 06_passport_stamps
```
THE PASSPORT. The lower pouch is covered with a scattered collage of cream line-art travel stamps and visa cachets at different angles (circles, rectangles, hexagons, dates, coordinates, small airplanes, coffee cherries). One big rectangular stamp in the centre reads ROAM. Playful wanderlust.
```

### 07_footsteps
```
THE WANDERER. A trail of small footprints walks from the bottom-left corner diagonally across the beans, loops once, and exits at the top right behind the label. ROAM is tiny, set in letterspaced caps at the bottom edge, as if the footsteps are the brand. Poetic, lots of negative space.
```

### 08_sadu_weave
```
THE WEAVE. A modern minimalist reinterpretation of Gulf Al Sadu Bedouin weaving: geometric bands of triangles, diamonds and zigzags in cream line weight, running horizontally across the lower half. ROAM sits big inside a clear horizontal band carved out of the weave, like a woven caravan banner.
```

### 09_horizon_sun
```
THE HORIZON. One single thin cream horizontal line crosses the pouch at two-thirds height (the horizon). ROAM sits on that line, with a large half-circle sun rising behind the letters made of fine concentric arcs. Below the horizon the beans are unprinted. Calm, cinematic.
```

### 10_halftone_dissolve
```
THE DISSOLVE. A halftone dot gradient: dense solid cream at the very bottom that dissolves into sparse dots as it rises, like beans settling or sand dunes. ROAM is huge, horizontal, printed across the solid base so it emerges from the dots. Contemporary, graphic.
```

### 11_compass_grid
```
THE COMPASS. Fine technical drawing: a large compass rose / navigation grid with degree ticks, latitude lines and N-E-S-W marks covering the lower bag. The four letters R-O-A-M replace the four cardinal points N-E-S-W around the compass. Clever, precise.
```

### 12_diagonal_tape
```
THE TAPE. Bold diagonal band — like airport baggage tape — wraps the pouch from lower-left to upper-right, solid cream with ROAM repeated in dark knocked-out letters along it, plus small arrows and barcode-like ticks. Energetic, streetwear-meets-specialty coffee.
```
